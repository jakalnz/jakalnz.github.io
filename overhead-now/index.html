About the data

Live positions come from adsb.lol, a network of volunteer receivers. Aircraft details and routes come from adsbdb.com. Some aircraft, like police or military, may hide their identity or not broadcast at all. Height above the horizon assumes you're near sea level. Track replay uses positions your relay has recorded, so it covers the times the app has been open, up to the last 2 hours.
